Our transparency principles

What we track

Whether a simulated email was sent, opened, clicked, credentials submitted, or reported, tied to a role or department, not used for individual punishment.

Who can see it

Each firm's own results are visible to that firm's compliance and IT security leads. Leaderboard standings are aggregate only.

What we don't do

We don't share one firm's raw results with another, and we don't use drill data for anything outside the awareness program.

Questions about how we operate?

The GPDS team is happy to walk through methodology, data handling, or anything else.
