Reporting & Dashboard

One dashboard, the same numbers for every firm

All campaign data funnels into a single PowerBI dashboard, giving each member firm and global leadership the same real-time view of results.

What's in the dashboard

Firm-Level Results

Sent, opened, credential-submitted, and reported rates broken down by department and by individual campaign.

Baseline vs. Retest

Side-by-side comparison showing measurable improvement after targeted training, per firm and per cycle.

Network Benchmarks

Anonymized comparison against other participating member firms, so leadership can see where they stand.

Accessing your dashboard

Each firm's compliance and IT security leads already have direct dashboard access. Results are also exportable to Excel for internal audits, cyber insurance documentation, or board-level reporting.

  • ✓Live PowerBI dashboard access
  • ✓Excel export for offline reporting
  • ✓Per-campaign and rolling history views
  • ✓Role-based access for compliance and IT leads
