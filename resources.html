Report a Phishing Email

Already clicked a link or entered your credentials?

Don't wait. Contact your firm's IT security team immediately. Acting fast matters more than getting every step right.

1

Don't click any links or open attachments in the email.

2

Don't reply to the sender, even to ask if it's legitimate.

3

Use your email client's "Report Phishing" button, or forward the email to your firm's security team.

4

If you're not sure whether something is a real threat or a GPDS drill, report it anyway. Reporting a drill email is exactly the right behavior.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GPDS?

The Global Phishing Drill Service (GPDS) is the team that runs standardized phishing simulations, training, and reporting across every member firm.

Will I get in trouble if I click a simulated phishing email?

No. Drills exist to build awareness, not to discipline anyone. A click just means that training gets routed to the people who need it.

How often does my firm run drills?

Baseline simulations run at least quarterly, with additional targeted retests depending on results. See the Drills & Events page for your firm's schedule.

Where can I see my firm's results?

Your firm's compliance and IT security leads have dashboard access. Details are on the Reporting & Dashboard page.

How is the leaderboard calculated?

Firms are ranked primarily by report rate, the share of employees who correctly flagged the simulated email, not click rate alone.

How do I join a webinar?

Upcoming sessions are listed on the Drills & Events page. Registration details will be added there as each session is confirmed.
