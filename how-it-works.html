How It Works

A repeatable four-stage cycle, run the same way for every firm

Each engagement follows the same methodology used internally to drill member firms globally, so results are comparable across the network.

01

Baseline Simulation

A controlled, no-warning phishing campaign runs across the firm to establish a documented starting point, using Microsoft Attack Simulator or an equivalent platform.

02

Target Identification

Results are analyzed to identify exactly which people and departments carry real risk, not a vague pass/fail score, but specific, actionable groups.

03

Employee Training

Focused training is assigned to the specific people who need it, addressing the exact behavior the simulation exposed.

04

Retest & Reporting

A follow-up simulation compares results against baseline, and outcomes are compiled into the shared dashboard as proof the risk is going down.

Data captured per campaign

Every simulation records timestamped events for each recipient, funneled into the PowerBI dashboard and exportable to Excel for local reporting needs.
